Bitcoin 10-day high / low (Quantpedia MAX/MIN)

Buy Bitcoin for a day whenever it closes at its highest or lowest close of the last 10 days. Highs tend to keep going and lows tend to bounce, so both are buys.

TypeShort-term trend + bounce, long only
CandlesDaily
TradesLong only, one day at a time (kept while the signal repeats)
SourcePadyšák & Vojtko (Quantpedia), “Trend-following and Mean-reversion in Bitcoin” (2022), re-checked by Quantpedia in 2024

The idea, and where it comes from

Quantpedia found that from 2015 to 2022 Bitcoin kept rising the day after it closed at a 10-day high (MAX, trend following), and also bounced the day after a 10-day low (MIN, mean reversion). Shorter lookbacks worked better than longer ones. Holding only on those days gave 98% a year with 48% volatility and a −38% worst drawdown. Their 2024 re-check found MAX still working after publication, while MIN had weakened since 2022.

The rules, exactly

  1. At each daily close: close ≥ the highest close of the last 10 days, or ≤ the lowest → buy at the next open.
  2. Hold one day; if the next close is again a 10-day high or low, keep holding.
  3. Long only, no stop.

Settings unchanged from the source. Binance USD-M futures for BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, BNB, DOGE and PEPE (5-minute tests: BTC and ETH) from 2019, or each coin’s listing, to 2 Oct 2026. Lab default costs: 0.05% fee and 0.05% slippage on every fill, about 0.2% a round trip, plus real funding. The average trade is in % of the position; t above 2 means it is unlikely to be luck. “Profitable after costs” needs a positive average with t ≥ 2 and a profit on most coins; “Edge before costs only” means the rule predicts something, but less than the trading costs.

On 28 coins it had never seen (every other coin with data: ADA, AVAX, LINK, NEAR, SUI, TAO … same settings, run once): 16 of 28 in profit, 6,822 trades, average +0.379% after costs (t +3.2), +0.622% before costs.

It passed: profitable after costs on all 7 coins, still positive since mid-2024, and again on 28 coins it had never seen (16 of 28 in profit).

But be clear what it is. It is long only, and crypto rose a lot over this period, so much of the profit is just being long. Compared with an average day for the same coin, its days were better by +0.43% on the 7 test coins (t 2.1), but only +0.15% (t 1.3, not significant) on the 28 fresh coins. It lost in 2022’s bear market. Treat it as a smarter way of being long crypto, with buy-and-hold-sized drawdowns at full size, not as a market-neutral edge.
