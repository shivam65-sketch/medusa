Supertrend

An ATR-based line that trails the price and jumps to the other side when a candle closes through it. One of the most used indicators in crypto, often traded as an always-in-the-market system: long when it flips up, short when it flips down.

TypeTrend following (stop and reverse)
CandlesAny; popular from 5 minutes to 4 hours
TradesLong and short, always in the market
SourceOlivier Seban; computed here exactly as TradingView’s ta.supertrend (checked value for value)

The idea

Take the middle of each candle, (high + low) ÷ 2, and draw bands 3 × ATR(10) above and below it. In an uptrend the lower band is the line, and it may only rise; in a downtrend the upper band is the line, and it may only fall. A close through the line flips the trend. It is a volatility-adjusted trailing stop that also tells you which way to face.

The rules, exactly

  1. Supertrend(10, 3) with Wilder-smoothed ATR, as on TradingView.
  2. When it flips up, buy at the next open; when it flips down, sell short. Each flip closes the old position and opens the new one.
  3. No stop by default: the opposite flip is the exit. Optionally the line can also act as a stop inside the candle.

A trend follower like the Turtle, and it behaves like one: nothing on 15-minute candles, a real but small edge before costs on 1-hour and 4-hour candles, not enough after costs.
